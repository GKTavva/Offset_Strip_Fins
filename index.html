Predict j & f Factor for Offset Strip Fins

Brief intro

This page implements the continuous numerical correlations (CFD-based) for offset strip fins from the referenced study. It accepts geometry and flow inputs and returns predicted Colburn j and friction f factors. The correlations are valid for gases/fluids with Prandtl number in 0.5–15.

Assumptions & Study Ranges

  • Steady-state, incompressible flow.
  • For LPD Case: Isothermal fin walls (Tw=315K) and inlet Tin=300K is used.
  • Reynolds range: 300 – 6000. Laminar ≤1500; transitional between 1500–2000 (treated as a linear interpolation between laminar and turblent); turbulent ≥2000.
  • Prandtl number applicability: 0.5 – 15 (gases & many liquids).
  • Surface roughness neglected; ideal metal fins assumed (aluminum in study).
  • Formulas below are empirical regressions derived using 1053 CFD data points. Hence, expect typical errors on order of a few % (see [1] for MAPE).
  • The ranges of fin height, length, thickness, and spacing for which the correlation is valid are detailed in the referenced study [1].
